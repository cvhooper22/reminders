Remember things by voice.

Say where you put something, who someone is, or what you need to do. Ask later and Siri tells you. Setup takes about two minutes.

1. Create your space

Only needed to open the locked tin in the app. There's no way to recover it yet, so pick one you'll remember.

Your key

Save this somewhere safe. It's your only login and it can't be recovered. Anyone who has it can read your notes.

2. Add the Shortcuts

Copy your key first. Each Shortcut will ask for it when you tap Add Shortcut; paste it there.

3. Teach Siri your phrase

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and find each Shortcut you just added.
  2. Tap its ⋯ menu, then Add to Siri (or rename it to the phrase you want).
  3. Say it out loud when asked. Apple requires that step to be in your own voice.

Then try: “Hey Siri, remember this.”
